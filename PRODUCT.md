# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Patients booking therapy/psychology sessions with Fundacja Niepodzielni — often anxious, in crisis, or otherwise vulnerable, booking solo without an account (guest flow, phone/email only). Context: quick, possibly stressed, on mobile or desktop, replacing a manual Bookero-calendar + bank-transfer/Instagram-story flow.

## Product Purpose

Self-service booking: search, hold a slot for 10 minutes, pay, get confirmation with video link/address immediately. Success = a calm, low-friction booking that removes the current manual back-and-forth, without feeling clinical or bureaucratic.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, human, trustworthy. Not sterile-medical (no cold blue/white clinic look), not generic-SaaS. The reference mockup (fundacja-niepodzielni.github.io/gabinet-makieta) sets the visual bar: cream background, confident navy headings, green as the single acting color for CTAs/status, soft pill tags, generous whitespace.

## Anti-references

Generic shadcn/default look (plain white cards, gray borders, no color, flat hierarchy) — this is what the app currently looks like and what we're moving away from. Also avoid cold clinical white/blue medical-portal aesthetics, and dense enterprise-dashboard density.

## Design Principles

- Calm over clinical: warmth and reassurance matter more than efficiency-flexing density, this is a mental-health service.
- One acting color: green carries all calls-to-action and positive status; navy carries identity/headings; nothing else competes for attention.
- Real hierarchy: overline label → bold heading → supporting copy, on every screen, not just the landing page.
- Low cognitive load: fewer, bigger decisions per screen (this matters more given some users are in distress or have ADHD).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No stated WCAG target; default to AA contrast minimums given the vulnerable/crisis-context user base. Respect reduced motion. Keep touch targets and form labels solid since the ADHD-diagnoza service explicitly serves users for whom low friction and clarity matter most.
